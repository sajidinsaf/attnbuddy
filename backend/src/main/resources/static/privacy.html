Brasstacks Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Brasstacks is a focus and productivity support app. Your privacy is fundamental to how we build this product. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what we never do with it.

Our Core Promise

Your personal data never leaves our controlled systems. We do not sell, share, or send your data to third-party analytics, advertising, or AI services.

What We Collect

DataWhy
Email addressAccount login and password recovery
Display nameShown in the app to personalize your experience
Tasks and notesCore app functionality — stored on our server so you can access them across sessions
Task completion timesUsed to learn your productive hours and improve task prioritization (on our server only)
Energy level (optional)Helps the app learn when you work best — you choose whether to log this
Device push tokenRequired to send you notifications (nudges, reminders)

What We Never Do

  • Send your task content, notes, or personal data to any third-party AI or cloud service
  • Track your behavior for advertising purposes
  • Sell or share your data with anyone
  • Include personal content in push notification text — notifications contain generic messages only
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs that send data to third parties

Rescue Mode Privacy

Any data from rescue mode (when you use the "I'm overwhelmed" feature) is treated as strictly private. It is never shared, never sent to any external service, and never used for analytics.

Push Notifications

We send push notifications through Expo's push service, which routes to Apple (APNs) for iOS. Notification messages are generic (e.g., "Your priorities today") and never contain your actual task titles or personal content. You can disable notifications at any time in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on our managed server infrastructure. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Authentication uses JWT tokens.

Data Retention

  • Your data is retained while your account is active
  • Deleted tasks are soft-deleted and permanently purged after 30 days
  • If you delete your account, all personal data is removed within 30 days

Your Rights

  • You can delete individual tasks at any time
  • You can request a full export of your data
  • You can delete your account and all associated data
  • You can disable all notifications (quiet hours or full opt-out)

Children

Brasstacks is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you through the app before the changes take effect.

Questions about privacy? Contact us at privacy@visibleai.com.
