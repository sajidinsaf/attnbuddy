Brasstacks Privacy Policy

Effective: July 30, 2026 | Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Brasstacks ("App") is provided by Visible AI ("Company", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

1. Our Core Commitment

Your personal data never leaves our controlled systems. We do not sell, share, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties. We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide and operate the App's features
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Prioritize your tasks using our scoring algorithm
  • Learn your productive patterns to improve recommendations (on our server only)
  • Send push notifications (nudges, reminders, weekly reviews)
  • Improve the App based on aggregated, anonymized usage patterns

4. AI and Data Training Transparency

We are explicit about AI:

  • Your data is NOT used to train any third-party AI models
  • Your data is NOT used to train our own AI models
  • If AI features are introduced in the future, they will be opt-in and will not process your personal content through external AI services
  • Task prioritization uses a deterministic scoring algorithm, not AI/ML

5. Rescue Mode Privacy

Data from rescue mode (the "I'm overwhelmed" feature) receives the highest privacy protection:

  • Never shared with any external service
  • Never used for analytics or product improvement
  • Never included in any data export except at your explicit request
  • You can delete rescue mode data at any time

6. Data Sharing

6.1 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

ServicePurposeData Shared
Expo Push NotificationsDelivering push notifications via Apple APNsDevice push token + generic notification text (never personal content)

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services that receive your personal data.

6.2 Push Notification Content

Push notification messages are deliberately generic (e.g., "Your priorities today", "Time for a check-in"). We never include your actual task titles, notes, goals, or any personal content in notification payloads. Task details are loaded on-device when you open the App.

7. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on our managed server infrastructure
  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds) — never stored in plain text
  • Authentication uses short-lived JWT access tokens with refresh token rotation
  • Database access is restricted to the application service only

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update your personal information
  • Deletion: Delete individual tasks or your entire account
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out of notifications: Disable all nudges via quiet hours, frequency settings, or device settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@visibleai.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Brasstacks is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

11. California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Right to delete your personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell your data
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

12. International Users

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States. We apply the same privacy protections regardless of your location.

13. Cookies and Tracking

The Brasstacks mobile App does not use cookies, web beacons, or third-party tracking technologies. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

14. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you through the App before the changes take effect. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
