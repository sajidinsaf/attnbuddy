Why I Built This

I lived most of my life not knowing I had ADHD. It wasn't until my mid-forties, when my brother — a psychiatrist — pointed it out, that things finally clicked. Decades of wondering why things that seemed effortless for others felt impossibly hard for me suddenly had an explanation.

By then I was running a company. Two teenage kids. A demanding market. Every day brings dozens of things that need attention, and every one of them feels equally urgent. The kind of life where dropping a single ball can have real consequences.

So I did what you'd expect: I tried everything. To-do list apps. Spreadsheets. Project management tools. Time-blocking. I read the productivity books. I tried the frameworks. They all had the same problem: they require the exact executive function skills that ADHD impairs. They hand you a list and expect you to prioritise, start, sustain attention, and remember to come back. That's not a tool — that's a test I was already failing.

I needed something different. Not another list. Not another system to maintain. I needed something that would think with me, not wait for me to think. Something that would answer the only question that matters when you're overwhelmed: what should I do right now?

So I built Brasstacks.

It shows you one task. The right task, right now. It breaks big things into small things. It sits with you while you work. And when everything feels like too much, it helps you breathe first and figure out the rest after.

It's not a cure. It's not therapy. It's a companion that understands that some days, just starting is the hardest part.

— Sajid, founder of Brasstacks

Important: Brasstacks is not a medical device, therapy replacement, or diagnostic tool. It is a productivity companion designed to support executive function. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

What It Does

One Task at a Time

No overwhelming lists. Brasstacks surfaces the single most important thing right now, using smart prioritization that weighs urgency, importance, deadlines, and your own patterns. You decide what to do with it — not what to do about everything.

Break It Down

Big tasks cause paralysis. Brasstacks helps you break them into micro-steps — small, concrete actions you can actually start. "Write the report" becomes "Open the document and type the first sentence." You can use templates or create your own steps.

Focus Companion

Sometimes you just need the sense that someone is there. Focus companion mode provides gentle check-ins while you work — like having a colleague quietly working alongside you. It's accountability without judgment.

Rescue Mode

For those moments when everything feels like too much. Rescue mode meets you where you are with simple options: do just five minutes on one thing, park it with a note for later, or take a moment to breathe and ground yourself first.

Context Memory

When you switch away from a task, Brasstacks asks where you left off. When you come back to it, that note is waiting for you. No more losing twenty minutes trying to remember what you were doing.

Gentle Nudges, Never Guilt

Customizable reminders with the tone you prefer — supportive, direct, or playful. Quiet hours when you need silence. And absolutely no streaks, no shame, no red "overdue" warnings. Missed something? It simply asks if you'd like to come back to it.

Learns Your Patterns

Over time, Brasstacks notices when you're most productive and adjusts its suggestions. It tracks your energy levels and completion patterns — all on our server, never shared with anyone — so the right tasks surface at the right times.

AI Assistant (Bring Your Own Key)

Ask the built-in assistant about your tasks, get help breaking them down, or just ask what to focus on. You connect your own AI account (Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini) — so you control the provider and the cost. Your API key is encrypted with your password, meaning only your login can unlock it.
