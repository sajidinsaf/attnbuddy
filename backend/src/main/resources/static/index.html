What is Brasstacks?

If you have ADHD, you already know that most productivity apps are built for neurotypical brains. They give you a to-do list, maybe a calendar, and expect you to just... do the things. That does not work when your brain fights you on prioritization, task switching, and getting started.

Brasstacks takes a different approach. Instead of showing you everything at once, it asks one question: What should you do right now? Then it helps you actually do it.

How It Helps

Single-Task Focus

No overwhelming lists. Brasstacks surfaces one task at a time using smart prioritization that weighs urgency, importance, deadlines, and your own patterns. You see what matters right now, not everything you have ever added.

Micro-Steps

Big tasks are paralyzing. Brasstacks helps you break them into tiny, concrete steps so you always know exactly what to do next. "Write the report" becomes "Open the document and type the first sentence."

Body Doubling

Sometimes you just need the sense that someone is there with you. Brasstacks provides gentle virtual companionship and accountability while you work, without judgment.

Rescue Mode

Feeling stuck or overwhelmed? Rescue mode helps you break through paralysis with guided prompts and breathing exercises. It meets you where you are, not where you think you should be.

Gentle Nudges, No Punishment

Brasstacks uses kind reminders with customizable tone and frequency. Missed a task? No guilt trips, no streak-breaking penalties. It simply asks if you would like to come back to it, or move on.

Your Data Stays Private

Your tasks, habits, and patterns are personal. Brasstacks does not sell your data, does not share it with advertisers, and does not use it to train AI models. Your information stays under your control.

Development Roadmap

Brasstacks is being built in phases, each one adding deeper support for executive function challenges.

Phase 1

Foundation

Core task capture, smart prioritization using the Eisenhower matrix combined with deadline awareness, and the "Right Now" single-task screen.

  • Quick task capture with natural language
  • Eisenhower matrix prioritization (urgent/important)
  • Deadline-aware scheduling
  • Single-task "Right Now" focus view
Phase 2

Focus Support

Tools to help you stay engaged once you have started: focus timers, micro-step breakdowns, and body doubling.

  • Customizable focus timer
  • Micro-step task decomposition
  • Body doubling companion mode
  • Session tracking and progress visibility
Phase 3

Emotional Resilience

Rescue mode for overwhelm, gentle nudge system, and adaptive tone that responds to how you are feeling.

  • Rescue mode with guided recovery
  • Customizable nudge frequency and quiet hours
  • Adaptive tone (cheerful, calm, direct)
  • No-guilt task transitions
Phase 4

Pattern Learning

Brasstacks learns your rhythms and adapts. It notices when you work best, which tasks you avoid, and adjusts its suggestions accordingly.

  • Time-of-day productivity patterns
  • Task completion trend analysis
  • Smarter priority suggestions over time
  • Personalized daily summaries
Phase 5

Sustained Growth

Goal setting, habit support, and long-term progress tracking to help you build momentum that lasts.

  • Goal setting and milestone tracking
  • Habit formation support
  • Long-term progress insights
  • Achievement recognition (without toxic gamification)
